# What's new: competitors compared at more towns

*For the GAIL sales and pricing team. Not yet released; this describes the version being prepared. It follows the Ex Works / Ex Depot release.*

## What you will notice

- **More competitors on Compare.** Before, a competitor appeared only if it published a price for your customer's exact town. Now, where it doesn't, Compare uses the zone the client-approved mapping assigns — so most towns show more competitors. Across the 313 towns, 188 gain at least one competitor; a town that showed no competitor at all can now show up to five.
- **Every card says how the town was matched:**
  - *No line:* the producer publishes a price for this town (**Exact Published Match**).
  - *"Territory Match: …"* (a quiet grey line): the producer's own published territory covers the town — HPL's district list, or a zone the producer prices for a whole state (for example IOCL's Kerala zone).
  - *"Inferred Location Match: priced at Udaipur, 95 km from ABU ROAD. Confirm before quoting."*: the producer publishes nothing for the town; the price is from its nearest approved zone. It says "across a state border" when that zone is in another state.
  - *"Retained Existing Mapping: … — the zone already in use."*: a mapping you already saw before this release, kept unchanged (60 of them).
- **"Basic price is from Gulbarga. No freight rate is published to BIDAR, so this producer is not compared."** Some producers (HMEL, HPL, OPaL) bill freight to the town's own name and publish no rate for it. The price is found, but no fair landed cost can be worked out, so the producer is shown but not ranked.
- **Deal** carries the same caveat, and a deal priced against an inferred zone is never shown with "high" data confidence.

## What changed for specific towns

- **Latur:** IOCL and HMEL are no longer compared. The zone used before was 312 km away; the approved review removed it rather than show a price that far away.
- **Bilaspur (Chhattisgarh):** HPL now prices from its Chhattisgarh zone. Before, it priced from a Himachal Pradesh district that happens to share the name — 1,121 km away.
- **HPL generally:** HPL's own territory list is now read correctly; a number of towns move to the HPL price point HPL itself assigns them.

## What did not change

- Ex Depot. A depot price is still only shown where the producer publishes a depot for that town — never borrowed from a nearby town.
- Prices, discounts and the price ladder. Only *which zone* a missing competitor is priced from changed, and only where the client-approved mapping says so.

## If a matched price looks wrong

Tell the pricing team the town, the producer and what the card said. The fallback can be switched off at once while it's checked; every inferred price then reads "no published price" again.
